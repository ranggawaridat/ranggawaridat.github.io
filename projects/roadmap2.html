Your first 90 days

DAYS 01—30

Design & foundation

Pilih problem, tulis product requirements, domain model, database schema, dan architecture diagram.

Build & reliability

Tambahkan background processing, worker, idempotency, retry, integration tests, dan simulasi kegagalan.

Deliverable

DAYS 61—90

Measure & improve

Load test, ukur latency dan throughput, analisis bottleneck, tambahkan observability.

Deliverable
Engineering case study dengan benchmark dan ADR.
